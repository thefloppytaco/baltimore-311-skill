# Baltimore 311 request types (verified against the live portal 2026-10-15)

Search term is what to type in the portal's "Submit a 3-1-1 Service Request" box.

## Pothole
Search: pothole. Department: Transportation. Usually resolved in 2 business days.
Questions: "Where is the pothole located?" (Street, Alley, Footway, Curb). "Is the problem due to a water meter, valve, manhole or steel plate?" (None of the above, Manhole, Steel plate, Valve, Water meter, Other).

## Missed trash / recycling
Search: missed trash. Opens "Missed Mixed Refuse Pickup". Department: Solid Waste. Usually resolved in 1 business day.
Questions: "Is your concern about a missed pick up today?" (Yes, No). "What time and day did you place your trash out for collection?" (date and time fields). "Has your neighbor's trash been collected?" (Yes, No). This is the only type that files without contact info.

## Bulk trash pickup
Search: bulk trash. Opens "Bulk Trash Pick Up-Weekday (No Appliances)". Department: Solid Waste. Resolution estimate varies.
Questions (both required): "What are your three bulk trash pickup items?" (textbox). "Where is your trash collected, front or rear?" (Front, Rear). Extra scheduling step with an Available Dates dropdown.

## Graffiti
Search: graffiti removal. Resolution estimate 3 business days.
The "Graffiti Complaint" flow forces a redirect popup to a subtype, so search "graffiti removal" and pick the matching subtype directly: public property goes to Graffiti Removal-Solid Waste, private property to Graffiti Removal (Private Property).
Questions: "Where is the graffiti located?" (Private property, Public property). "Is the graffiti gang related?" (Yes, No, Do not know). "Is the graffiti offensive?" (Yes, No, Do not know). "What type of surface is it?" (dropdown: Asphalt, Brick, Cement, Marble, Metal, Painted Wall, Plastic/rubber/vinyl siding, Stone, Stucco, Wood, Other, Do Not Know).

## Street light out
Search: street light. Opens "Street Light Issue". Department: Transportation. Usually resolved in 4 business days.
Questions: "Where is the light located?" required (Front, Rear, Side, Park or playground). "What is the problem with the light?" (dropdown: Single light out in block, All street lights in block out, Light constantly on, Light going on/off (cycling), Missing globe, Light inadequate or too bright, Other). "Enter the location details:" (textbox). "How long has the light been out?" required (textbox). "Have you reported this before?" (Yes, No, Do not know). Tip: the pole number from the metal tag on the pole speeds this up.

## Damaged, lost, or stolen trash / recycling cart
Search: damaged cart. Opens "City Trash Can or Recycling Can Issue". Usually resolved in 10 business days.
Questions: "What is the trash can/recycling cart concern?" required (dropdown: Damaged, Removal/Pick-up, Received 35 Gallon/ want 65 Gallon, Received 65 gal cart-want 35 gal, New to Location, Stolen/Lost). Answering Damaged reveals: "Do you need trash or recycling cans?" required (Recycling Can (Blue), Trash Can (Green), Both). Each address gets one free replacement cart.

## Sidewalk repair
Search: sidewalk. Opens "Footway/Sidewalk Repair". Department: Transportation. Usually resolved in 3 business days.
Questions: "What is the problem with the sidewalk?" (textbox). "Is sidewalk damage due to a water repair?" (Yes, No). "Is a handicapped ramp affected?" (Yes, No). "Do you consider this hazardous condition?" (Yes, No). None are marked required. Tip: flag tripping hazards, they get priority.

## Dirty street / alley
Search: dirty street. Opens "Dirty Street Cleaning" (sibling: Dirty Alley Cleaning). Department: Solid Waste. Usually resolved in 7 business days.
Questions: "Was the trash dumped from a vehicle, small hauler, or contractor?" (Yes, No). "Is there 100 pounds or more of trash and debris?" (Yes, No). "Is the debris obstructing traffic?" (Yes, No). "Please describe the debris in the street?" (textbox).

## Clogged storm drain
Search: storm drain. Opens "Storm Inlet Choke". Department: Water/Wastewater. Usually resolved in 7 calendar days.
Questions: "What is the nature of the problem?" (textbox). Tip: note pooling or flooding water.

## Something else
Use the portal's "Submit General Request" when no type matches. The portal asks one to three short questions for the chosen type.
